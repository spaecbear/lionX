Side quests of the Archive

  The Tarred Chests     tar: pack whole rooms into one chest, and unpack them
  The Rewriting Desk    sed: change words in a thousand books at once
  The Two Copies        diff: find exactly where two versions of a story part ways
  The Far Libraries     ssh: speak to Lexicons in archives far from here
