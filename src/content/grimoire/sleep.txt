NAME
    sleep - wait

SYNOPSIS
    sleep NUMBER[SUFFIX]...

DESCRIPTION
    Sleep does nothing for NUMBER seconds (or m minutes, h hours). It is
    the gentlest way to practice jobs: `sleep 600 &` starts a spirit that
    does nothing, quietly, in the background.

    While a spirit runs in front of you, Ctrl+C ends it and Ctrl+Z
    freezes it into a job.
