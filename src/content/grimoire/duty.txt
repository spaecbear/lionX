NAME
    duty - ask for restoration work

SYNOPSIS
    duty

DESCRIPTION
    The Corruption keeps creeping back, so there is always something to
    mend: a misplaced scroll, a journal to check, a hidden note to find.
    Duty asks Quill for the next task, or reminds you of the one at hand.
    Duties use only spells you know, so they are good practice.

    Duty is a spell of the Lexicon itself; real terminals do not have it.
