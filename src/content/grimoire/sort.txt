NAME
    sort - put lines in order

SYNOPSIS
    sort [OPTION]... [FILE]...

DESCRIPTION
    Sort prints the lines of all FILEs, or standard input, in order.
    By default the order is by character code: capitals before small
    letters, and "10" before "9".

    -n, --numeric-sort   order by number, so 9 comes before 10
    -r, --reverse        largest or last first
    -u, --unique         print each distinct line once
    -f, --ignore-case    fold small letters into capitals
    -b                   ignore leading blanks

EXAMPLES
    sort names
    sort -rn scores
    sort words | uniq -c | sort -rn     the most common words first
