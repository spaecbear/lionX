NAME
    settings - how the Lexicon looks and moves

SYNOPSIS
    settings
    settings theme NAME
    settings motion on|off|auto
    settings reader on|off

DESCRIPTION
    With nothing after it, settings shows your preferences.

    theme    candlelit (amber), cyber (green), electric-blue, high-contrast,
             and themes you earn or find in play
    motion   off stops Quill's typing and the drifting spirits; auto
             follows your device's reduced-motion setting
    reader   on is for screen readers: no ASCII art, no animation, and
             new output is announced

    Settings is a spell of the Lexicon itself, available everywhere.
