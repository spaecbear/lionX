NAME
    top - watch the spirits, live

SYNOPSIS
    top [-b] [-n COUNT]

DESCRIPTION
    Top shows the busiest processes first, by share of CPU, and redraws
    every second. The spirit hogging the Archive is always near the top.
    Press q to leave.

    -b         print once (or COUNT times with -n) instead of redrawing
    -n COUNT   how many times to print in -b mode
