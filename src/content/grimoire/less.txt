NAME
    less - turn the pages of a long book

SYNOPSIS
    less [-N] [FILE]...

DESCRIPTION
    Less shows a book one screen at a time, so long texts don't rush past.
    It reads FILE, or standard input when you pour something into it.

    Inside the book:
      space, f        next page
      b               previous page
      j, Enter, Down  next line
      k, Up           previous line
      d, u            half a page down or up
      g, G            first or last page
      q               close the book

    On a touch screen, swipe up and down to scroll.

    -N     number each line

EXAMPLES
    less journal.txt
    ls -l | less
