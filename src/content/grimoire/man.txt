NAME
    man - open the Grimoire to a spell's page

SYNOPSIS
    man SPELL

DESCRIPTION
    The Grimoire holds a page for every spell you can learn. Each page
    gives the spell's true name, how to cast it (SYNOPSIS), what it does,
    and its options. Pages open in the same reader as less: space for
    the next page, q to close.

    In the SYNOPSIS, words in [brackets] are optional, and ... means
    you may give more than one.

EXAMPLES
    man ls                read about ls
    man man               read this page

EXIT STATUS
    0 if the page was found, 16 if there is no such page.
