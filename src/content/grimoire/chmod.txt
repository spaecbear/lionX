NAME
    chmod - change the wards on a book or room

SYNOPSIS
    chmod [OPTION]... MODE FILE...

DESCRIPTION
    Every book and room has nine wards in three sets: what its owner
    (u) may do, what its group (g) may do, and what everyone else (o)
    may do. Each set allows reading (r), writing (w), and executing or
    entering (x). `ls -l` shows them: -rwxr-x--- means the owner may do
    everything, the group may read and run, and others may do nothing.

    MODE can be symbolic:
      u+x       give the owner execute
      go-w      take writing away from group and others
      a=r       everyone may read, and nothing else
      u=rw,o=   owner reads and writes; others get nothing
    A missing u/g/o/a means all three, but leaves alone anything your
    umask protects.

    Or MODE can be three octal digits, one per set, adding r=4, w=2,
    x=1: 755 is rwxr-xr-x, 640 is rw-r-----, 700 is rwx------.

    -R, --recursive   change rooms and everything inside them
    -v, --verbose     report every change
    -c, --changes     report only actual changes

    Only the owner of a book (or root) may change its wards.

EXAMPLES
    chmod +x ritual.sh          make a script runnable
    chmod 750 private-room      owner all, group read and enter, others none
