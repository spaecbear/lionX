NAME
    renice - ask a spirit to take less

SYNOPSIS
    renice [-n] PRIORITY [-p] PID...

DESCRIPTION
    Every spirit has a niceness, from -20 (greedy) to 19 (generous).
    The nicer a spirit is, the less of the Archive's strength it takes,
    and the more it leaves for everyone else. top shows it in the NI
    column.

    You may make your own spirits nicer. Only root may make a spirit
    greedier, or change someone else's.

EXAMPLES
    renice 10 4321
    renice -n 19 -p 4321
    sudo renice -n -5 -p 4321
