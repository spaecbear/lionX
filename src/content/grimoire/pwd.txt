NAME
    pwd - print the name of the room you stand in

SYNOPSIS
    pwd [-LP]

DESCRIPTION
    Pwd speaks your full location in the Archive, starting from the
    Foundation Stone (/) and naming every hall on the way to where you
    stand. Lost? Cast pwd before anything else.

    -L     print the path as you walked it (the default)
    -P     print the true path, with no shortcuts (the same here)

EXAMPLES
    pwd                   prints something like /home/apprentice
