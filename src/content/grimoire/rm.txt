NAME
    rm - unmake books (and rooms) for good

SYNOPSIS
    rm [OPTION]... FILE...

DESCRIPTION
    Rm removes each FILE. There is no undo: nothing goes to a bin, and no
    spell brings it back. Cast it carefully, and cast `ls` first.

    -r, -R, --recursive
           remove rooms and everything inside them
    -f, --force
           do not complain about missing books, never ask
    -i     ask before every removal
    -d, --dir
           remove empty rooms
    -v, --verbose
           say what is being removed

    Rm refuses to remove . and .., and will not operate recursively on
    the Foundation Stone (/) at all.

EXAMPLES
    rm stray-page             one book
    rm -i *.lx                ask about each one
    rm -r old-shelf           a room and all it holds
