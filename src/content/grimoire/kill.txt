NAME
    kill - send a signal to a spirit

SYNOPSIS
    kill [-SIGNAL] PID|%JOB...
    kill -l

DESCRIPTION
    Kill sends a signal to each process. By default it sends TERM, a
    polite request to stop. Most spirits obey. Some do not.

    -9, -KILL   cannot be ignored: the spirit ends at once
    -STOP       freeze it in place (it can be resumed)
    -CONT       let a frozen spirit continue
    -l          list the signals

    You may only signal your own spirits, unless you borrow root's
    authority with sudo. %1 means job 1 from `jobs`.

EXAMPLES
    kill 4242
    kill -9 4243
    kill %1
