NAME
    hint - ask Quill for help with what you are doing

SYNOPSIS
    hint

DESCRIPTION
    Quill gives help in three steps: first a nudge, then the name of the
    spell you need, then the whole spell written out. Each cast of hint
    goes one step further.

    Hint is a spell of the Lexicon itself. Real terminals don't have it,
    but they do have man pages, and so do you.
