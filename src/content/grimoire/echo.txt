NAME
    echo - speak a line of text into the Archive

SYNOPSIS
    echo [-neE] [STRING]...

DESCRIPTION
    Echo repeats each STRING back, separated by single spaces, and ends
    with a newline. It is the simplest spell, and one of the most useful:
    paired with > it writes words onto the page.

    -n     do not end with a newline
    -e     understand backslash escapes such as \n (newline) and \t (tab)
    -E     do not understand backslash escapes (the default)

EXAMPLES
    echo hello            prints: hello
    echo -e "a\tb"        prints a and b separated by a tab
