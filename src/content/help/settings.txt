Usage: settings [NAME VALUE]
Show your preferences, or change one:
  settings theme NAME        the Lexicon's colors (??? are still hidden)
  settings motion on|off|auto
  settings reader on|off     screen-reader mode
  settings music on|off
  settings volume 0-10
  settings song NAME|auto    a secret song you found, or each wing's own
  settings easy on|off       hints and solution no longer slow your progress
